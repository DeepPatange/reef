    The fibre

    Dense Nile Delta fibre, dried by the sun.

    Luffa aegyptiaca grows on trellises at Field 7 in Damanhour and is picked in September, when the skin turns light. Twenty-one days in the open air, then peeled and seeded by hand. Never bleached: the colour is the sun’s. Every batch is graded before it is cut, and Grade A means 58 fibres per square centimetre.

    The soap

    Sabon Ghar, cured slowly in the Levant.

    Olive oil and laurel oil, poured, cut and left to cure until the bar is firm and long-lasting. Laurel 16 carries 16% laurel oil for every day and sensitive skin; Laurel 30 is the stronger bar for oily skin. Work it into the fibre, not onto your skin.

    The ritual

    Two minutes, one area at a time.

    Wet the loofah for 30 seconds until it softens. Lather. Small circles for two minutes, lighter where skin is thin, firmer on the back. Rinse, then hang it by the cotton tag, out of the spray. Dry fibre lasts longer.
