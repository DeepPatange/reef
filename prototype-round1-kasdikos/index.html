The ritual · two minutes

Wet. Lather.
Circle. Hang to dry.

  1. 01 · WetSoak the loofah under warm water for 30 seconds. It softens as it drinks.
  2. 02 · LatherWork the Sabon Ghar into the fibre, not onto your skin.
  3. 03 · CircleSmall circles for two minutes, one area at a time.
  4. 04 · HangHang it by the cotton tag, out of the spray. Dry fibre lasts longer.
