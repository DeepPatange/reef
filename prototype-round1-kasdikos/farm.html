Al Reef & Laurel

The countryside, and the laurel.

Al Reef means the countryside: the Nile Delta farmland where our loofahs grow on trellises at Field 7, Damanhour. Laurel is the oil in our Sabon Ghar, the soap of Levantine hammams.

We begin
with the field.

Gourds climb the trellises from March and hang to arm’s length by late summer. We pick in September, once the skin turns from green to brown and light.

The sun
does the rest.

Twenty-one days drying in the open air on the farm. Skins come off by hand, seeds are saved for next spring, and nothing is bleached. The colour you see is the sun’s.
