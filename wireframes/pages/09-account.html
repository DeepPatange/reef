09 · Your ritual · /account/rituals
Your ritual

Where renewing customers manage their ritual without friction. Arrives from the day-50 WhatsApp link, “Account” in the header, order and shipping messages, and the batch page. No action is forced: Ship now, Skip and Swap sit side by side, and cancelling is always visible, never buried.

Sections, top to bottom
01Account nav — greeting, founding-member line, seven links (active = Basalt left bar) and a WhatsApp help card. Sticks while the page scrolls.
02Active ritual card — the set, its cycle and price, the Fade bar (day 43 of 60) and the hanging tag at the same day. Five equal action pills. The cut-off date and the cancel link sit right under them.
03In your next refill — what ships, with tag colours, refill price, delivery address and card. “Edit refill” opens quantities in a dialog.
04Add to your next refill — three one-tap add-ons that ride along with free delivery. They join that refill only; the bag line offers “Add to every refill”.
05Coming up — the next three refills on a timeline, each with a Skip toggle and its own cut-off date.
06The Year of Rituals — the one upsell, on Basalt, with the arithmetic and the refund rule in plain sight.
07Orders and WhatsApp and email — recent orders with invoices; three message toggles.
Motion and interaction
M10The Fade — on load the bar fills to the real day (900 ms) and the tag’s ink settles to the same day. Not scroll-linked here: it is a status, not a story.
M13Inline updates — Skip, Swap, Pause or a Skip toggle update the card in place: dates and prices roll to the new value (300 ms), then a quiet confirmation line with “Undo” replaces the cut-off caption for 6 s.Lula: Horizon variant-picker pattern
M18Dialogs and sheets — Swap soaps, Pause and Cancel open as centred dialogs on desktop and bottom sheets on mobile (400 ms, 38% Basalt scrim). Esc, the close button and the scrim all close them; focus is trapped inside.Lula: Horizon dialog
Mobile
—Account nav becomes horizontal tabs that scroll sideways; “Your ritual” is first.
—Action pills in two columns at 44 px; “Change frequency” runs full width because it is the longest label. Every action opens a bottom sheet (see “Skip sheet”).
—Coming up becomes a vertical timeline; orders become two-line rows.
Content and data
—Dates run in a true 60-day chain: 31 Aug → 30 Oct → 29 Dec 2027 → 27 Feb → 27 Apr 2028. Day 43 of 60 = 12 Dec 2027. Cut-off = ship date minus 5 days (24 Dec, 22 Feb, 22 Apr).
—Year of Rituals: 6 × AED 149 = AED 894; prepaid AED 769 saves AED 125 (14%).
—Pause: 1, 2 or 3 months move the next ship date by 30, 60 or 90 days (2 months: 29 Dec → 27 Feb).
—Data comes from the renewal app’s customer portal API (Recharge or Skio); Shopify customer accounts for orders, addresses and cards.
Open questions
CheckSpec dates were off by a day (31 Oct + 60 = 30 Dec; 2028 is a leap year, so 27 Feb + 60 = 27 Apr). Shown corrected; confirm the date engine.

CheckCut-off rule: 5 days before ship? Does “Ship now” charge today and restart the 60-day clock?
CheckPrice impact of soap swaps (Laurel 30 is AED 10 more than Laurel 16 as a single). Does the refill stay AED 149?
Check“Every 90 days” contradicts the tag (fades by day 60). Offer it only inside the cancel flow?
