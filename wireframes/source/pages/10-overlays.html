10 · Bag, menu and modals · overlays
Overlays and touchpoints

Everything that opens over a page: the mega menu, the bag, search, the founding-list invitation and the day-50 WhatsApp that keeps a ritual renewing. Each layer does one job, carries at most one Silt button and closes the same way: ✕, Esc or a click on the scrim.

Overlays, one by one
01Mega menu — opens from “Rituals” on hover (150 ms intent delay) or on click/Enter. All seven sets with one-time prices, the singles, one featured set and the Build-your-own tile. Navigation only, so no primary button.
02Bag drawer — opens after Add to bag (M14) or from “Bag (n)”. Each line names its cycle; one quiet nudge moves the one-time soap onto the renewal. Primary: Checkout · AED 123.
03Founding list — the site’s only marketing pop-up: no timer, no stock counts, no “hurry”. Primary: Join the list. The Silt panel is the screen’s one accent; حصاد (hasad, “harvest”) is this page’s one Ruqaa word.
04Search — opens from the header icon or the “/” key with focus in the field. Popular searches before typing; results from 2 characters, matched on names and on what’s inside each set. Enter opens the full results page.
05Mobile menu — full screen from the left: big links in Cormorant, service links, region at the foot.
06Mobile bag — full-height sheet from the bottom. The list scrolls; Subtotal and Checkout stay pinned.
07Mobile founding sheet — half-height bottom sheet, same copy and rules as 03, no image so the form clears the keyboard.
08Day-50 WhatsApp — the renewal touchpoint, outside the site. One message per cycle; the quick replies change the next refill in Recharge/Skio and show on 09 Your ritual. Neutral mock: the live chat uses WhatsApp’s own interface.
Motion and interaction
M18Drawers, sheets, dialogs — mega menu and search drop 8 px and fade in (300 ms), columns 40 ms apart. Bag slides in from the right, mobile menu from the left, sheets from the bottom (400 ms, calm out). The founding dialog fades and rises 16 px (400 ms). Scrim fades to Basalt 38% (300 ms). Closing reverses in 300 ms.Lula: Horizon menu-drawer, cart-drawer, dialog
M14Add to bag → drawer — the button reads “Added” with a tick (400 ms), then the drawer opens and the header count ticks to 2. The new line starts on Dune and settles to Steam over 1200 ms so the eye finds it.Lula: Horizon atc-slide-in
M13Move to renewal — the Clay nudge turns Laurel 30 into a renewal line: AED 55 rolls to AED 44, the subtotal to AED 112, and the button label follows (300 ms). Undo shows for 5 s.
M15Menu hover — the hovered set gains an arrow that slides in 8 px; the featured card crossfades to that set (300 ms).Lula: hover-image-v2.js
M19Search results — update as you type (150 ms debounce); cards crossfade in place, no layout jump.
Day 50Renewal touchpoint — sent on day 50 of each cycle, mid-morning local time (in this sample, Sun 19 Dec 2027). “Ship it” confirms Wednesday’s shipment; “Skip this one” moves the refill on 60 days; “Swap soaps” offers 2 × Laurel 16 · 2 × Laurel 30 · keep both. No reply means it ships as planned.
Focus, Escape and scrims
·Bag, search, founding list, mobile menu and sheets are modal dialogs (role “dialog”, aria-modal, named by their title). Focus moves in on open (bag: its title · search: the field · founding list: the email field · menu: the first link), stays trapped while open and returns to the trigger on close.
·The mega menu is a disclosure, not a dialog: aria-expanded on “Rituals”, arrow keys move through the links, tabbing past the last link closes it, and it closes 300 ms after the pointer leaves.
·Everything closes with ✕, Esc or a click or tap on the scrim. Sheets also close on a downward swipe past 30% of their height; the founding list also on “No thanks”.
·Page scroll locks while a drawer, sheet or dialog is open (scrollbar width reserved, so nothing jumps).
·Scrim: Basalt at 38%. The mega menu and search keep the header above the scrim (they belong to it); the bag and the founding dialog dim the whole page.
·Reduced motion: overlays fade only (150 ms), no slides.
Founding list: when it appears
·Once per visitor, after 30 s on the site or 50% scroll of a page, whichever comes first.
·Never on checkout, the bag, account or batch pages (QR visitors already own a loofah), and never on a product page before something is in the bag.
·Not shown to founding members or signed-in customers. Closing it any way, or joining, stops it on that browser; the offer stays in the announcement bar.
·Joined state, same dialog: “You’re on the list.” + “Your 25% code comes with our launch email.” + btn-secondary “Keep browsing”.
Mobile
05Each big link opens a second level that slides in from the right with a Back row (Rituals: the seven sets with prices; Field notes: the five pillars).
06Add-ons scroll sideways, the next card peeking in. Free delivery is said once, at the top; the pinned footer keeps only Subtotal, Checkout and the renewal line. Payment marks move to checkout so Checkout stays in view on a 390 × 844 screen.
07Email field uses the email keyboard; WhatsApp uses the phone keypad with +971 preselected. The sheet rises with the keyboard.
08Customers can also type “skip”, “swap” or “pause”; anything else goes to a founder.
