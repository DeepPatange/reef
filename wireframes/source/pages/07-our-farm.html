Our farm

The origin story and the proof behind it: where the loofah grows, how it is made, who handles it and how it reaches the Gulf. Visitors arrive from the header and footer, the “Traced to Field 7” band on product pages, Field notes and Instagram. The page earns trust first and sells once, at the end: Shop the Duo · AED 85.

Sections, top to bottom
01Header: announcement + header, “Our farm” active.
02Hero: coordinates rail, “Field 7, Damanhour.”, one line of place and process, then the panoramic trellis image. Centred, quiet, no button: the page is a read.
03Manifesto: one display sentence revealed word by word. Sets up the promise the rest of the page keeps: every step, shown.
04Why we started: founders’ hands with a dried gourd, then the founder story (placeholder) and the one Ruqaa word, الريف, with its meaning.
05From seed to shower: eight steps from planting to Day 01 in your shower, as a pinned horizontal timeline with a step index above it.
06Field 7, in numbers: the four proof points (21 · 58 · 60 · 0) on the page’s one Basalt band, each with a line on why it matters.
07The hands behind Field 7: three portraits of hands at work, captioned with task, name and role.
08And the soap?: the Sabon Ghar half of the ritual, on Dune, with a link to the soaps.
09The route: Field 7 → Alexandria → Jebel Ali, with Riyadh dashed as the next stop.
10Field notes: three articles, one per content pillar, so the story keeps going after this page.
11CTA band: the page’s only primary button, with the renewal price beneath it in Clay.
