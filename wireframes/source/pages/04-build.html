Build your ritual

For people who want their own mix: who it’s for, loofahs, soaps and a cycle, with the bundle saving updating as they go. Arrives from “Build yours” in the header, the Basalt tile on Rituals, the mega menu and the Duo page. The one primary action: Add ritual to bag · AED 195.

Sections, top to bottom
01Intro and step pills — headline, one line of promise, and four pills that show progress (done, current, to come). Tapping a pill scrolls to that step.
02Who’s it for? — four choice cards. Picking one presets quantities and tag colours (Two of us = the state shown). Nothing is locked; every step stays editable.
03Choose your loofahs — six item cards with steppers. Cards with a quantity of 1 or more get the Basalt selected border. Kids’ pieces carry an age badge. Tag colours are chosen per pad, so nobody borrows the wrong one.
04Choose your soaps — same card pattern: Laurel 16, Laurel 30, Shaped Trio, Guest Bar.
05How often? — cycle chips (60 days preselected) plus a three-part “what happens next” strip: today, day 50, day 60. Teaches the renewal before the customer pays.
06Your ritual (summary) — sticky card: line items, value, bundle saving, today’s price, the renewal price, a four-tier saving bar and the primary button.
07Or start from a set — four sets on Dune. “Load this set” fills the steps and scrolls back to step 1. The Pair is flagged “Closest to yours” because the current build is The Pair minus its soap rest.
Motion and interaction
M06Sticky summary — the summary column sticks 24 px below the top once the header hides (M03) and releases at the end of step 4. The step pills follow the step at viewport centre.Lula: hover-image-v2.js pattern (sticky column + active list)
M13Options and totals — a stepper or chip change sets the card border in 200 ms; value, saving, today and renewal figures roll to the new number (300 ms); the button label updates with the price; the tier bar fills segment by segment.Lula: Horizon variant-picker
M14Add ritual to bag — button shows “Added” with a tick (400 ms), then the bag drawer slides in with the ritual as one grouped line.Lula: atc-slide-in, cart-drawer
M18Mobile summary sheet — “Review” in the sticky bar opens the summary as a bottom sheet (400 ms, drag to dismiss).
M07Mobile set cards — horizontal swipe with momentum.
Mobile
—Steps stack. Who’s it for becomes a 2 × 2 grid; item cards become full-width rows with 44 px steppers; the cycle chips become full-width option rows (easier to hit with a thumb than wrapped chips).
—A sticky bottom bar (“5 items · AED 195 · save 23%” + Review) appears after step 2 and hides when the in-page summary scrolls into view.
—The full summary sits at the end of step 4 with the one primary button; the same content opens as a sheet from the bar (frame “Summary sheet”).
Content and data
—Arithmetic shown: 44 + 44 + 65 + 45 + 55 = AED 253 value. 23% saving = AED 58. Today AED 195. Renewal AED 156 = 195 × 0.8, the same 20% renewal step as the Duo (85 → 68).
—Tiers: 2 items 5% · 4 items 15% · 5 items 23% · 7 items 28%. Only loofahs and soaps count; the nudge line always names the next tier.
—Rules: at least 1 loofah and 1 soap, otherwise the button is disabled with a hint. “A gift” defaults the cycle to Just once and adds a card message in the bag. The first box carries the caddy box and ritual card; refills are loofahs and soaps only.
—Built on Shopify bundles + Recharge or Skio; the summary is one bundle line in the bag, not five products.
