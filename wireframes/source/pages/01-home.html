0130 seconds
Wet.

Soak the loofah under warm water for 30 seconds. It softens as it drinks.

0220 seconds
Lather.

Work the Sabon Ghar into the fibre, not onto your skin. Ten turns is plenty.

032 minutes
Circle.

Small circles, one area at a time. Lighter where skin is thin, firmer on the back.

Home

The front door for every audience, built to sell one thing first: The Duo with renewal switched on. Most visitors arrive from Instagram, TikTok and creators, so the page proves the product (origin, ritual, renewal) in the order a customer meets the brand, then widens the basket (sets, gifts, kids). Primary action: Shop the Duo · AED 85.

Sections, top to bottom
01Hero — Field & Marble: headline left, the Window right holding a slow loop of a wet loofah (stock 02), a hanging tag at Day 01. One Ruqaa word: ليفة. The only Silt text is “not made.”
02Manifesto — the purpose line, revealed word by word.
03The Duo module — Lula’s homepage buy box, ours: renewal preselected, fibre and soap chips, “Which fibre is mine?” helper. Lets Instagram traffic buy without leaving the page.
04Three proofs — Rooted (trust) · Renewed (hygiene) · Returned (planet), from the messaging framework, each linking deeper.
05Specimen — the content pillar as a section: one backlit slice, six facts. Basalt field, the page’s dark moment.
06Comparison — loofah vs plastic pouf vs supermarket mitt; our column raised.
07The two-minute scrub — sticky image + four steps (Wet, Lather, Circle, Hang).
08From the field — the one full-bleed image on the site (guidelines), Field 7 coordinates.
09Renewal — the Fade, the Day 0/50/60 timeline, three ways to buy, “Start your ritual”.
10Rituals carousel — all 7 sets, drag to browse, quick add on hover.
11Gifting — split panel (Lula’s claims panel) for Ramadan, Eid and weddings.
12Notes from the first showers — reviews carousel, hidden until real reviews exist.
13Field notes — the journal, one card per content pillar.
14Footer — “Letters from the Delta”, links, Instagram row, expanded lockup.
Motion and interaction
M01Hero momentum — first scroll is damped (40% → 0 across the hero), so the hero is felt, not skipped.Lula: hero-momentum.js (60%)
M02Window opens — oval scales .94 → 1, footage settles 1.08 → 1 over 1200 ms; headline lines rise 24 px, 120 ms apart.
M03Header — hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up; Steam + hairline after 80 px.Lula: Horizon sticky header
M20Tag sway — the hanging tag sways ±2° for three loops, then rests.
M04Word reveal — words go 20% → 100% as the paragraph crosses the viewport.Lula: text-animation.js
M07Gallery, carousels, reviews — drag/swipe with momentum, arrows and a progress line; reviews keep the centre card large.Lula: product-slider.js, review-slider.js
M13Options — switching One-time ↔ Renew rolls the prices and updates the button label.
M14Add to bag — “Added” tick, then the bag drawer slides in (page 10).
M09Line draw — the three proof icons draw their strokes on enter (900 ms).
M05Scrubbed specimen — slice turns with scroll speed, reverses on scroll up; callouts fade in left then right.Lula: video-spinning-smooth.js
M06Sticky ritual — image sticks; each step turns solid at viewport centre and the image crossfades.Lula: hover-image-v2.js
M11Field parallax — image at 0.85× scroll, 1.1 → 1 scale.
M10The Fade — ink lines fade one by one and the day count ticks 01 → 60 as you scroll through Renewal.
M15Hover — card image 1.04 zoom + “Quick add” pill rises; journal titles underline.
M08Slow marquee — gift promises drift upwards; the centre line is solid; pauses on hover.Lula: marquee.js
Mobile
·Window first, then headline and one full-width primary button (Lula puts its button above the headline; ours stays under it).
·Specimen callouts become a 2 × 3 grid; comparison becomes “ours vs one” with a chip to switch the competitor.
·Sticky ritual: the image sticks under the header while the four steps scroll beneath it.
·Option to test: a slim sticky “Shop the Duo · AED 85” bar once the hero scrolls out.
Content and data
·Prices from the strategy (estimates until landed cost). Renewal pricing shows on every set.
·“Now shipping · Field 7 · Harvest Sept 2026” and the specimen numbers should come from the live batch data, not be typed into the theme.
·“Founders’ pick” replaces “Bestseller” until there is sales data.
