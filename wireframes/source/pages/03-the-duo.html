03 · The Duo · /products/the-duo
The Duo

The hero product page, built to convert with renewal already selected. Visitors arrive from Home (“Shop the Duo”), the Rituals grid, Instagram and TikTok ads, and the batch page (“See the Duo”). Primary action: Add to bag · AED 68 (renew every 60 days). Everything below the buy box answers one doubt each: where it’s from, what’s inside, how to use it, why renew.

Sections, top to bottom
01Gallery + buy box — Lula’s product arrangement: a big stage (800 × 960) and a 2 × 2 grid on the left, a 520 buy box on the right that sticks while the gallery scrolls. Order in the box: name → price → story → spec rail → how to buy → cycle → fibre → soap → add. Renewal is preselected; one-time and The Year of Rituals are one tap away.
02Traced to Field 7 — proof of origin. The receipt previews the batch page that the tag’s QR opens (page 08).
03What’s in the box — five things, tag and card included, so the price reads as a set rather than a sponge.
04Spec sheet — every claim in one table; PDF for press, hotels and retailers.
05The two-minute scrub — the ritual in four steps, with the 0:45 video from the gallery.
06Choose your fibre and soap — explains the two choices in the buy box.
07Renewal (Basalt) — the tag at day 41 and the timeline: why renewal exists, in the brand’s own words.
08Notes from the first showers — reviews, designed now, hidden until there are 20+ verified ones. The sticky add-to-bag bar is drawn over its top.
09Questions, answered — six product questions; WhatsApp for the rest.
10Pairs well with — singles to add (one-time). If the Duo is on renewal, the bag drawer offers “Add to your renewal instead” (page 10).
Motion and interaction
M07Gallery — arrows step the stage through 6 slides (crossfade 600 ms); the counter updates. Mobile: swipe with momentum, dots + counter. Thumbnails below the stage open the same slide.Lula: product-slider.js (1 / 3 counter on the buy box image)
M13Options — the Basalt border moves (200 ms); the big price and the button label roll to the new value (300 ms). One-time hides “How often” and reads “Add to bag · AED 85”; The Year reads “Add the year · AED 349”.Lula: subscribe-and-save radios; Horizon variant-picker
M14Add to bag — the button shows “Added” with a tick (400 ms), then the bag drawer slides in from the right (page 10). Same on the sticky bar.Lula: atc-slide-in, cart-drawer
·Sticky buy box — the box (about 1,640 px) is taller than most screens, so it scrolls with the page until its end is on screen, then pins 24 px from the bottom while the rest of the gallery passes (sticky-sidebar pattern); on taller screens it pins to the top. Add to bag stays in view until the gallery ends; then the sticky bar takes over. Sticky bar — slides down (300 ms) once the buy box’s button scrolls out of view; hides again when the footer arrives. On mobile it sits at the bottom of the screen and hides while the in-page button is visible.
M09Map draw — coast, Nile branches and the dashed route draw in 900 ms; the Field 7 pin lands last.
M10The Fade — scrubbed with scroll: the tag’s three ink lines fade and the day count ticks 01 → 41 as the section passes. Reduced motion: shown at day 41.
M17Accordions — buy box (Spec sheet, How to use, Delivery and returns, Ingredients) and FAQ; one open at a time, 300 ms.Lula: accordion-custom.js
M07Reviews — drag carousel with arrows and a progress line.Lula: review-slider.js
M15Pairs well with — card hover; “Add” turns into “Added” and opens the drawer.
Mobile
·Gallery first (390 × 488, swipe), then the buy box stacked. “How often” becomes a three-way segmented control; the button sits beside a compact stepper.
·Sticky bottom bar drawn at the end of the first screen (y 764–844), over the description, as it appears on load.
·What’s in the box, reviews and Pairs well with swipe sideways; ritual steps 2 × 2; renewal timeline turns vertical.
Content and data
·Prices: AED 68 per delivery (20% off AED 85), AED 85 one-time, AED 349 for the year (6 × 68 = 408). Estimates until landed cost.
·Prices are always IBM Plex Sans, never Cormorant (guidelines). The buy-box price is Plex Medium 32/40 desktop, 26/34 mobile; product names stay in Cormorant.
·Closed FAQ answers. How long does one loofah last? About 60 days of daily use, which is why the tag fades when it does. Tag fades early? Message us and we’ll ship the refill early. Keep it fresh? Rinse, squeeze, hang it by the tag out of the spray. Skip or cancel? Any time before the next ship date. Saudi Arabia? From April 2027.
·Review names, quotes and the 4.9 / 128 summary are sample content.
