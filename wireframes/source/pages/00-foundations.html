Motion, unhurried.

Taken apart from lulaoil.com’s live code (Shopify Horizon 3.1 plus custom scripts), then slowed down to fit “quiet pride in doing it properly.” Codes like M04 appear as blue markers on every page.

Durations
Micro · hover, colour150 ms
UI · toggles, accordions, header300 ms
Reveal · fade-up, crossfade600 ms
Draw · line icons, counters900 ms
Cinematic · hero load1200 ms
Easing
Calm out (entrances)cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
Standard (state changes)cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1)
Scroll-linkedlinear, scrubbed
Fade-up distance24 px
Stagger80–120 ms
Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: no scroll resistance (M01), manifesto shown in full (M04), specimen shows a still frame (M05), tag shown at day 41 (M10), no parallax (M11). Everything else becomes a 150 ms opacity fade.

CodePatternBehaviour · timingLula source
M01Hero momentumScroll is damped while the hero is on screen, easing from 40% resistance to none by the hero’s bottom edge. Feels deliberate, not stuck.hero-momentum.js (uses 60%; we use “mild” 40%)
M02Window opensOn load the Window scales .94 → 1 and the photo inside settles 1.08 → 1 (1200 ms, calm out). Headline lines rise 24 px, 120 ms apart.Our own, replaces Lula’s static hero
M03HeaderSticky. Hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up (300 ms). After 80 px it gains a Steam background and hairline.Horizon sticky header, transparent → solid
M04Word revealStatement text starts at 20% opacity; words turn solid one by one as the paragraph scrolls through the viewport (400 ms per word, scroll-linked).text-animation.js
M05Scrubbed specimenA backlit loofah slice turns as you scroll; playback follows scroll speed (0.7–1.9×) and reverses when you scroll up. Callouts fade in around it.video-spinning-smooth.js (the spinning bottle)
M06Sticky storyImage column sticks; each numbered step turns solid at viewport centre (inactive 45%) and the image crossfades (600 ms).hover-image-v2.js + mobile-sticky-image.js
M07Drag carouselDrag or swipe with momentum (velocity × 0.95 per frame), grab cursor, arrow buttons and a progress line for keyboard and mouse users.product-slider.js, review-slider.js
M08Slow marqueeLines drift slowly; on hover playback eases to a stop over 500 ms and resumes on leave.marquee.js
M09Line drawBrand icons and maps draw their stroke when they enter (900 ms), then stay still.Our own, from the icon construction
M10The FadeThe tag’s three ink lines fade one after another and the day count ticks 01 → 60 as the section scrolls (scrubbed).Our own, the renewal story
M11Field parallaxThe one full-bleed image moves at 0.85× scroll and settles from 1.1 to 1 scale.Horizon video/image background
M12Count-upNumbers (21 days, 58 / cm², 60 days) count up once when they enter (900 ms).stars-count.js pattern
M13Price and option changeSelected card border and fill 200 ms; prices roll to the new value (300 ms); the button label updates with the price.Horizon variant-picker
M14Add to bagButton shows “Added” with a tick (400 ms), then the bag drawer slides in from the right (400 ms) over a 30% Basalt backdrop.Horizon atc-slide-in, cart-drawer
M15HoverLinks: underline grows left to right (300 ms). Nav: 70% → 100% opacity. Cards: image 1.04 zoom (600 ms) and a “Quick add” pill rises 12 px.Horizon hover + Lula nav (0.4 s)
M16Pinned timelineThe farm’s seed-to-shower track pins and moves sideways as you scroll down; a Silt progress line fills.New, extends M06
M17AccordionHeight eases open 300 ms; plus turns to minus.accordion-custom.js
M18Drawers and sheetsMenu slides from the left, bag from the right, mobile sheets from the bottom (400 ms); links stagger in 40 ms apart.Horizon menu-drawer, dialog
M19Filter reflowCards re-order with a FLIP animation (400 ms) when a chip filter changes; no page reload.Horizon facets
M20Tag swayThe hanging tag on the hero Window sways ±2° on a 4 s loop. The one playful moment; stops after 3 loops.Our own (“one light twist per piece”)
Not adopting from Lula
✕ The countdown-timer discount pop-up. Our voice rules out urgency tricks; the founding offer appears once, calmly, with no timer. ✕ All-caps hero headlines. Caps belong to the wordmark and the spec rail; headlines stay in sentence-case Cormorant. ✕ Full-bleed hero photo. The guidelines reserve the one bleed for the farm image; the hero uses the Window. ✕ Mono typeface, bright blue and yellow. Our information face is Plex Sans; colour stays Silt, Ecru, Basalt, Kraft.
Build notes
Scrubbed video (M05): short H.264 + WebM, every frame a keyframe, poster image, loads only when near the viewport. Lula keys out the white background of its bottle video on iOS with a canvas; shoot our specimen on a true Basalt field instead. All motion off-main-thread where possible (CSS transforms and opacity only). No scroll-jacking beyond M01.
