Batch page

The page behind the QR code on every cotton tag. People arrive from a phone camera, often holding the loofah: the buyer on day 1, a gift recipient, later a hotel guest. It proves origin, teaches the ritual and asks for one thing, a WhatsApp number for the day-50 reminder. That reminder is how one-time buyers and gift recipients become renewals. Primary action: Remind me on day 50. Mobile is the primary design; desktop follows it.

Sections, top to bottom (mobile)
01Minimal header: no announcement bar and no promotions. Menu, logo, “Shop”. Hand-built from the header classes because the macro has no “Shop” variant.
02Hero: batch code rail, “Your loofah grew here.”, the Window on Field 7 at harvest, coordinates as its caption.
03Receipt: the batch record as a slip with a dashed top edge, nine rows. This is the proof, so it sits straight under the image.
04Day tracker: “Day 01 of 60”, the tag at today’s fade, the bar with day 50 marked, the drying tip, then the two-minute scrub in four steps.
05Reminder: the only primary button. One field, one promise: one message, nothing else.
06When the tag fades: cut, compost, keep the box, plus a diagram of the box folding into a caddy.
07Gift card: for recipients. The Duo as a secondary button, so the reminder stays the main ask.
08Mini footer: logo, Instagram, WhatsApp, domain.
+Reminder set (state frame): the reminder card after a successful submit. It confirms the date and number and offers change and cancel.
Motion and interaction
M02Window opens: on load the Window scales .94 → 1 and the photo settles 1.08 → 1 (1200 ms); the coordinates caption fades in after.Our own; replaces Lula’s static hero
M10The Fade, live: here it shows the real day, not a scroll scrub. On load the bar fills to today and the tag’s ink lines fade to match (900 ms, once). Day 01 shown.Our own, the renewal story
M09Line draw: the box-to-caddy diagram draws its folds one after another as it enters (900 ms each).Our own, from the icon construction
—Reminder submit: the button shows a spinner (max 1 s), then the card crossfades to “Reminder set” (300 ms) and the page does not jump. On error, an inline line under the field: “That number doesn’t look right. Check the country code.”
M18Country code: the +971 chip opens a bottom sheet (400 ms) on mobile, a dropdown on desktop; +966 is listed for Saudi Arabia from April 2027.Lula: Horizon dialog
—Everything else fades up 24 px (600 ms). No momentum, no parallax: this page should load fast on hotel Wi-Fi and in a bathroom.
Desktop
—For people who type the URL from the card or open a shared link. Header with no active item; announcement dropped to match mobile.
—Left (6 cols): Window on a Steam stage, receipt, gift card. Right (5 cols): tracker, reminder, afterlife. The columns end level, so nothing needs to stick.
Content and data
—The QR on each tag opens /batch/{code}. It identifies a batch, not a single loofah, so the day count is kept per device from the first scan, or from the delivery date when the visitor is signed in.
—Receipt rows come from one batch record (Shopify metaobject): field, coordinates, planted, harvested, drying dates, grade and density, graded by, packed date and place, soap batch. Missing rows hide rather than show blanks.
—Signed-in renewers see the “Reminder set” state in place of the form (their day-50 message is already scheduled).
—WhatsApp via Bitespeed: one opt-in template on day 50. One-time buyers get the renewal offer in that message; renewers get “Your tag is fading. A fresh one ships Tuesday.”
—Two-minute scrub copy is the ritual card’s; step 04 drops “Dry fibre lasts longer.” because the tip just above already says it.
