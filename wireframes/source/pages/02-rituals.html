02 · Rituals · /collections/rituals
Rituals

The collection of all seven sets, plus a way out for anyone who doesn’t fit one (Build your own). Visitors arrive from the header, the home “Rituals” carousel, Instagram and search ads (often with a filter preset, e.g. ?for=a-gift). Primary action: open a set. Quick add is the shortcut for people who already know.

Sections, top to bottom
01Collection header — names the idea (every set renews) before any price. Window IMG 06 + Ruqaa طقس as the page’s one Arabic word.
02Filter row — “Who’s it for?” chips map to the audiences in Brand Strategy (self-carer, couples, families, kids, gifters, grooming). Renewal-price toggle and sort sit right; hairline below.
03Grid 4 × 2 — 7 sets + the Basalt “Build your own” tile in slot 8. Card: 4:5 Dune image with cut-out circles, max one badge, name, contents, value struck through + price, renewal price in Clay. Gift sets say “Gift set · no renewal”. This is the stacked card variant (322 wide); Home uses the 400-wide variant with the price on the name row.
04How renewal works — three steps that answer “what am I signing up for?” before the compare table. Links to the full explainer.
05Compare the sets — the five renewing sets side by side. Gifts (First Bath, Hammam Chest) are left out because they don’t renew.
06Questions, answered — five pre-purchase questions, first one open; WhatsApp to the founders for the rest.
Motion and interaction
M19Filter reflow — single-select chips; cards re-order with FLIP, 400 ms, no reload. URL updates (?for=two-of-us) so filtered views can be shared and used in ads. Build your own always stays last.Lula: none (single product); Horizon facets
M13Renewal-price toggle — prices roll to the renewal price (300 ms); the struck value hides and the second line reads “AED 85 one-time”. Remembered for the session.Horizon variant-picker price roll
M15Card hover (The Pair shown) — image 1.04 zoom over 600 ms; “Quick add · AED 199” rises 12 px. Quick add uses the defaults (renew every 60 days, Gentle, Laurel 16) and opens the bag drawer (M14), where fibre and soap can be changed.Lula: product-slider hover; Horizon quick-add
M09Icons draw — box, tag and renew icons draw their stroke as the band enters (900 ms, 120 ms apart).
M17Accordion — one open at a time; height eases 300 ms; plus turns to minus.Lula: accordion-custom.js
M18Filter sheet (mobile) — slides up 400 ms over a 38% Basalt scrim; drag down, tap the scrim or the close button to dismiss. The button count updates live (“Show 3 sets”).Horizon dialog / menu-drawer
M07Compare swipe (mobile) — the table scrolls sideways with momentum; the label column stays pinned at the left edge.Lula: product-slider.js
Mobile
·Chips scroll sideways under the title; “Filter and sort” opens the bottom sheet (frame 3), where the same chips wrap.
·2-column grid (169 wide). Seven sets leave an odd one, so The Hammam Chest becomes a full-width feature card, followed by Build your own at full width.
·No hover on touch: tapping a card opens the set. No quick add on mobile in round 1 to keep cards calm (test later).
·Compare table swipes; renewal band stacks with 40 px icons.
Content and data
·All prices from Brand Strategy and still estimates until landed cost. Struck value = the same pieces bought singly.
·Badges on Dune images use the Steam fill (“Ages 3–10” is badge-dune, which would vanish on Dune).
·Sort options: Recommended (Duo first, then by audience size) · Price, low to high · Biggest saving.
·Closed FAQ answers. What ships on renewal? Fresh loofahs and soap only; the first box is already your caddy. Can I skip or pause? Yes, any time before the next ship date, on WhatsApp or in your account. Outside the UAE? Saudi Arabia from April 2027, then the rest of the GCC. Plastic-free? Yes: unbleached FSC kraft, twine and cotton; no plastic, no lamination.
