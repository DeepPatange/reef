Gifts

Seasonal gifting: Ramadan and Eid first, then weddings, new babies and corporate thank-yous. The Gifter lands from search (“Ramadan gift Dubai”), the announcement bar and Instagram. One primary action: Shop gift sets (scrolls to the grid). Corporate buyers get their own enquiry form further down. Dates appear as information only: no countdowns, no “order now”.

Sections, top to bottom
01Hero (Basalt, the page’s one dark band) — seasonal headline beside the Hammam Chest in a dark Window. Swaps with the calendar: Ramadan → Eid → wedding season → year-round “thank-yous”.
02Find the gift — 2 chip rows, For and Budget; Anyone and Any budget selected by default.
03Gift grid — 6 gifts, 3 × 2. The Pair is drawn in its hover state. One badge per card at most.
04Every gift includes (Dune) — the 4 service promises that make us easy to give: card, wrap, date, no prices.
05Inside the Hammam Chest — the flagship gift unpacked, 6 pieces; “Also at Ounass” appears here only.
06Corporate gifting — from 50 sets; 3 steps plus an enquiry form that goes to the founders’ inbox and the CRM.
07FAQ (Steam) — 5 questions, first open.
Motion and interaction

M19Filter reflow — For and Budget combine (AND); cards re-order with FLIP (400 ms), the count updates. A new baby → First Bath; The team → links to Corporate.Horizon facets
M15Card hover — image zooms 1.04 (600 ms), label gives way to “Quick add · AED 199”. Gift sets add as one-time by default.Horizon quick-add

·Form — inline validation on blur (work email, +971 number); the date picker greys out dates inside the lead time. On send, the card is replaced by a thank-you note in place (no page change).
Mobile
·Window first, then headline, then one full-width primary, above the fold at 844.
·Chip rows scroll sideways and run off the right edge to show there is more. Grid is 2 × 3.
·Chest contents become rows; the form stacks to one column with the primary at full width.
Content and data
·Prices are Brand Strategy estimates. The Year of Rituals: AED 349 vs 408 (save 14%), non-refundable after the first box ships; say so at checkout.
·Closed FAQ answers to write: delivery date (from how many days ahead), price (“No. Prices stay off the slip and the card; the receipt comes to you.”), gifting a renewal, Ramadan delivery hours.
·Ruqaa word كَرَم (karam, generosity) is a proposal for the page’s one Arabic word; confirm with an Arabic copywriter.
·Ramadan and Eid dates are estimates that depend on the moon sighting; the line updates each season.
