Little Reef

The kids range (5 pieces) and the 2 sets built for parents. Parents arrive from the Little Reef nav link, The House on Rituals, parenting creators and the monthly bath-time episode. One primary action: Shop the Starter · AED 99. Tone: calm and careful. Every safety statement on this page is either true today or flagged.

Sections, top to bottom
01Hero — Window (Camel Mitt) with the kids tag hanging off its edge. Ruqaa صغير (“little”) is the page’s one Arabic word. Primary: Shop the Starter; secondary scrolls to Shop by age.
02Shop by age — 5 pieces, 1 row of 5 on desktop. The age badge is the card’s one badge. Soft Pebble is drawn in its hover state.
03Sets (Dune) — Little Reef Starter (renews) and First Bath (one-off gift), as wide Steam cards with contents listed so parents see what’s in the box.
04The camel tag (the page’s one Basalt band) — the Little Reef version of the Fade. Explains renewal without the word “subscription”.
05Bath time, two minutes — the 4-step ritual cut to 3 for children, plus the monthly bath-time episode (content pillar).
06Our promises (Steam) — 4 promises. 2 are planned, not done, and stay hidden until they are true.
07Parents ask — FAQ, first item open, WhatsApp link for everything else.
08For the whole house — cross-sell to The House for families where grown-ups and children share the bathroom.
